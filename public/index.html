Questions

Before you
enroll.

Can I take the Hands-On Academy before I finish the online course?
Yes. You can attend any academy date after you enroll online. You'll need to complete both parts before your Certificate of Completion is issued.
How long until I can get my hardship license?
Most students qualify within about ten days of completing both parts. You take your certificate to the DMV to apply.
Can I pay in installments?
Yes. Both the online course and the bundle can be split into three monthly payments at checkout.
What do I bring to the Hands-On Academy?
Your learner's permit, closed-toe shoes, and a parent or guardian signature if you're under 18. We provide the vehicle.
